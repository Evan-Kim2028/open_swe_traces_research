The dataset, and what it cost

A unit is certified when it fails at L0 and passes at L2 or higher — hard, fair, solvable and verifiable. The rung it needs is its difficulty: a unit that only flips at L5 is harder than one that flips at L2, not a failed task. Certified units are the product; trials are the bill.

Does each decision make a task cheaper to mine?

Marginal cost: trials spent per unit certified in that hour. The cumulative average (8.39) drags a whole day of history behind it and cannot show a change working. The green line is the theoretical floor of 2.6 — one L0 trial to prove hard, one L2 to prove solvable, plus screening overhead. Bars above the floor are the waste still being paid.

Sources: every reward.txt and result.json under experiments/dose_response/jobs, replayed in mtime order by backfill_history.py. Decision times are pinned, not read from file mtimes, because editing a script would otherwise redate the decision it implements. Regenerated each supervisor tick.
