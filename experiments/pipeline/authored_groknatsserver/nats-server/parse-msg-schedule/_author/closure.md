The message-schedule pattern parser was stubbed. It mixes three dialects (`@at` RFC3339, `@every` durations, cron plus named aliases) with extra house rules: timezone pointers are forbidden on `@at`/`@every`, intervals under one second are rejected, and a missed fire is skipped forward to now-plus-interval. A solver cannot recall the alias table or the one-second floor from the function name; those only exist in this server's scheduler.
