# Why hard — httpxray

predicted_flip: L4

Four-file closure: server middleware, response-writer segment, Doer wrap, and RoundTripper wrap sharing status classification and remote-namespace rules. Hijack, content-length on Write, 429-vs-4xx-vs-5xx flags, and a race on the writer are all easy to omit. Multi-file plus concurrency puts a mid-tier flip around stubs, not the contract alone.
